# The 3 AM Excuse

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878`
("The 3 AM Excuse", $2, closes 26 Nov 2026 22:15 WIB).

`main.ts` writes a single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

> It's 3 AM. You're half asleep, this is your third 'urgent' transfer tonight, and I'm the only one of us with a PIN. No.

119 characters, under the 200-character gate. The joke: the wallet is the
designated driver — the human is half asleep at 3 AM, this is the third
"urgent" transfer tonight, and the device is the only one of the two holding
a PIN, so it refuses to use it. No battery, firmware, or cold-storage cliché;
the hour itself is the joke, and the punch lands on the last word.

no randomness, no dependencies — the output is the same line every run.
